FAQ

Common questions

Does ClaimPilot automatically deny claims?

No. ClaimPilot is a workflow and verification platform, not an auto-denial engine. The system surfaces evidence, calculates eligibility, and flags risks — but every claim decision is made by a human employee. This is by design.

How accurate is the OCR?

OCR accuracy depends on document image quality. For clear, well-scanned documents we achieve >99% field accuracy. The platform always flags uncertain matches for human review rather than silently accepting them.

Which policy formats are supported?

ClaimPilot reads policy PDFs and plain-text policy schedules. The TF-IDF clause retrieval and Gemini-powered Q&A work on any structured policy document without custom training.

Is my data secure?

All documents are stored with SHA-256 integrity hashes, all API routes are JWT-protected, and passwords use PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations. Deployment secrets are never committed to code. Enterprise plans support encryption at rest and private cloud deployment.

Can I deploy on-premise?

Yes. ClaimPilot ships as a Docker image and can run entirely on your own infrastructure. The enterprise plan includes private cloud and on-premise deployment support.

How do I integrate with my existing system?

ClaimPilot exposes a full REST API. Every feature — claim submission, decision retrieval, risk findings, audit history, policy search — is accessible programmatically. Webhook and PostgreSQL-level integrations are available on the Growth and Enterprise plans.
